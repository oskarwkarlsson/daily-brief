ScienceScience & CS

  • Axiom’s Lean formalization of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups, announced Oct 26, is about 43.1M lines built in 24 days by AI agent swarms. Its 34 deviations include a gap in Walter’s Unbalanced Group Theorem, fixed with a new “preservation of simple tops” theorem. The code is public. Axiom, GitHub
  • Over a quarter of fetal cortical neurons carry large copy-number changes, against about 6% after birth. That suggests the brain culls billions of mutant neurons around birth (Shao et al.). Nature
