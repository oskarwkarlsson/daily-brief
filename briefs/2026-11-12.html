ScienceScience & CS

  • Nature: CSIRO researchers re-imaged M87* with GenDIReCT, a diffusion model trained on non-astronomy images that uses only calibration-free closure invariants. They recover the ring in every good EHT dataset (2017, 2018, 2021) at 42.2 ± 1.5 μas, which implies 6.5 ± 0.5 billion solar masses. That settles a long dispute over whether the ring was a processing artifact. Nature
  • arXiv (posted Nov 9): Google DeepMind and Quantum AI had teams of Gemini 4 Carbon agents write quantum programs that are formally verified in Lean, sharing results through a wiki and "conferences". They cut costs 290x for fixed-point arcsin and 11x, 8x and 2.5x for fusion stopping power, photochemistry and P450 drug metabolism, all still above early fault-tolerant hardware budgets. arXiv
