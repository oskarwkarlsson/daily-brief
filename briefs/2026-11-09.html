  • On Nov 8 the Houthis hit the Aramco office tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District. The Saudi energy ministry says falling shrapnel caused minor damage and no casualties, but BBC Verify footage shows at least one floor badly hit. The Houthis also claimed strikes on King Khalid airport, Aramco tanks in Riyadh and Abqaiq. Saudi forces said they intercepted at least four missiles over Nov 7 and 8. BBC, ISW

ScienceScience & CS

  • Nature: NVision Quantum built a single-molecule spin-photon interface. Carbene molecules in a host crystal at 4.5 K kept spin coherence for about 2.2 ms, and their optical frequency stayed stable for more than an hour. That makes chemically designed molecules a candidate quantum-network node. Nature, Phys.org
  • Nature (Nov 4): a programmable neutral-atom quantum simulator with up to 180 qubits observed prethermal dynamical phase transitions and "resonant melting" after sudden quenches. These regimes are hard to simulate classically in high dimensions. Nature
  • arXiv (Princeton): Scientist World turns papers into executable research environments, with 1,000 verified so far. Frontier AI agents beat the reproduced paper method in 84% of improve attempts and 74% of from-scratch attempts. A Qwen3-8B ideator trained on the traces reached the frontier cohort's median. arXiv
