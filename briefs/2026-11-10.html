ScienceScience & CS

  • NVIDIA researchers (arXiv) argue "You don't need long context": with frequent compaction and selective file reading, Claude Opus 4.5 and GPT 5.6 Sol keep accuracy on SWE-bench, Terminal-Bench and long-document tasks while live context stays at 16K to 48K tokens. Serving at 256K instead of 16K costs 3.6x to 7x more per token on open models. arXiv
  • Cambridge, QIMR Berghofer and AstraZeneca: a transient mRNA encoding Myc and Cyclin T1 made heart muscle cells divide after a heart attack in mice, lifting blood pumped per beat by about 10%, with gains lasting 12 weeks. Published in Nature Cardiovascular Research. Cambridge
  • arXiv: CPU-ARC turns LLM reasoning traces into reusable caches that run on CPU, claiming frontier-level results on hard math and science at 16x to 291x lower cost (not yet read in full). arXiv
