ScienceScience & CS

  • Quantum: a Nature Physics paper (SUSTech and International Quantum Academy) shows autonomous error correction keeping a cavity-encoded logical qubit alive for about 2.0 ms, 18% beyond the best physical qubit in the system, using engineered dissipation instead of measure-and-feedback; it also gave a 6.3 dB sensing gain. Nature Physics, Quantum.world
  • Astronomy: JWST detected water and CO2 ice for the first time in Beta Pictoris's debris disk (Science). The ice is mostly amorphous and uniform across the disk, pointing to constant low-speed collisions among icy bodies, which also explains the puzzling CO gas there. Phys.org (Science paper)
  • Cosmology: a Barcelona team finds big bang nucleosynthesis still matches observed light-element abundances even if electron and positron masses differed in the first minutes, leaving room for early CPT violation with possible links to matter-antimatter asymmetry and the Hubble tension. Scientific American
