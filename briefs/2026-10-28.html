ScienceScience & CS

  • ETH claims: OpenAI posted a paper and Lean proof on Oct 26; Anthropic posted its own about six hours later, saying a Claude research model found a deterministic 2^o(n) 3-SAT algorithm. OpenAI also claims proofs of local smoothing in all dimensions and 4D Yang-Mills existence, which mathematicians want released. Treat all as unverified. OpenAI GitHub, Ryan Williams, Times of AI
