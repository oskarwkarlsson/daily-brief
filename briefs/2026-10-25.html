ScienceScience & CS

  • Nature: prime editing was used inside a human body for the first time. Prime Medicine treated the first person in its Wilson's disease trial by correcting a mutation in liver cells that stops them clearing copper; first results are due next year. Nature
  • Nature Physics: autonomous quantum error correction beat the break-even point in a logical qubit encoded in the spin states of a single trapped ion, with no syndrome measurements or real-time feedback. Nature Physics
  • Nature: Pinal, a 16B-parameter model, designs functional proteins from plain-language descriptions. Separately, all-antiferromagnetic tunnel junctions showed a long-assumed-impossible spin-torque switching effect with 363% magnetoresistance at room temperature, a path to denser magnetic memory. Pinal, AFM junctions
