AIAI build-out & tools

  • Huang returns: Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, president and director. It cited his "candor" and input from senior US government officials, Colette Kress goes back to CFO, and the independent investigation will "proceed to conclusion". The company is also creating a Chief Compliance Officer role and a national security committee. Trump says he "convinced" the board and that Huang "did nothing wrong". Reuters notes opposition from lawmakers, and several outlets (relaying a report) say Huang told the board about more affairs before the vote. Nvidia, Reuters, Hindustan Times
  • Export controls: Commerce put Singapore's Megaspeed (where Alice Huang was CEO) and six related firms in Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand on the Entity List, with a presumption of denial for licences. Reuters says an Alibaba unit in Singapore, Apex, was added too; Alibaba denies any diversion. Singapore's PM Wong says the Megaspeed probe continues. CNBC, Straits Times
  • Build-out: Applied Digital signed a 150 MW, 18-year lease with an unnamed investment-grade hyperscaler, worth about $5.4bn in base-term revenue and delivering in H1 2028. That takes its contracted base-term revenue above $41bn. Applied Digital, Reuters
  • New tools: Z.ai's GLM-5.4 is out, its first "Loop Transformer". It runs 28 layers of the 750B GLM-5 base twice and claims 2T-3T-class quality with a 1M context, with API and open weights still to come. Anthropic launched Claude Ship, which reviews pull requests, fixes failing checks and approves routine changes by policy (Team and Enterprise, partly beta). OpenAI shipped a chat-tuned GPT-6.2 Sol to paid ChatGPT plans. With Cisco it also open-sourced Shirudo, an eBPF runtime that shields running Linux services until a patch lands. Z.ai, TechCrunch, The Verge, OpenAI

Red SeaRed Sea shipping

  • Red Sea itself: there has still been no corroborated attack on commercial shipping since Aug 24 (JMIC Update 116). Bab el-Mandeb traffic is about 23 transits a day, roughly 22% below pre-blockade levels, and the threat level is "substantial". The Houthi warning to evacuate ships from Saudi Red Sea ports stands, but carriers are still calling at Jeddah. Nothing new since Monday's Rabigh refinery claim, which Saudi Arabia has not confirmed. JMIC/UKMTO, Forwarder News (unfamiliar site)
  • Hormuz: the IRGC says three "offending" tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines (one near the southern coast, two in the eastern strait) despite US air cover and escort. There is no independent confirmation yet. The US now has three carriers enforcing its blockade of Iranian ports, and Treasury sanctioned more than two dozen Iranian exchange houses and front companies Monday. CNBC, Al Jazeera
  • Cyber at sea: the WSJ reports that the US is investigating cyberattacks on as many as 50 tankers carrying oil, LPG and chemicals, some with bomb threats or ransom notes. Iran is the primary suspect and Russia a possible one. Reuters could not verify the report. Reuters/gCaptain, WSJ

ScienceScience & CS

  • Quantum advantage, verified: IBM and UChicago ran 70 logical qubits (2,415 logical two-qubit gates, 468 T gates) for a sampling task that leading classical methods can't practically reproduce. It took about 15 minutes, and an error-detecting circuit design puts a statistical lower bound on fidelity. Logical error rates were 10x below physical. Gambetta: "firmly in the quantum advantage era". ScienceDaily/IBM, arXiv
  • AI for algorithms: Google DeepMind teams of Gemini 4 agents, with every result proved in Lean, cut quantum circuit costs by 11.8x for an inertial-fusion calculation, 2.5x for the drug-metabolising enzyme P450 (481 to 333 qubits) and 290x for an arcsin routine. The Lean library is open source. Google DeepMind
  • Chemistry: in Nature, Merck shows continuous N-to-C peptide synthesis from unprotected amino acids (the way ribosomes build proteins), with about 95% yield per coupling. It cuts isolations about 10x and waste up to 100x, and the team made semaglutide and degarelix with it. Nature
