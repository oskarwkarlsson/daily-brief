ScienceScience & CS

  • Science (Microsoft AI): "Sequential diagnosis with language models". The MAI-DxO orchestrator simulates a panel of doctors that asks questions, orders tests and revises the diagnosis. It scored 83.2% on NEJM case challenges and 80.3% on MIMIC-IV ED cases, beating participating generalists in text-only tests; real-world validation is still needed. Science, Nadella
  • Nature (Weizmann and others): a current-driven helicene molecular rotor turns in one direction 100% of the time, and the rotation boosts its conductance. A step toward electrically driven molecular machines. Nature
  • Science, quantum materials: the first electronic "bound states in the continuum" in a 2D metal (Pd5AlI2), where quantum interference traps electron waves near defects even though they sit inside the metallic continuum. Two companion papers image the quantum melting of Wigner crystals and Chern insulators in moiré materials. Science, Columbia
