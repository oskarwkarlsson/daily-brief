He expects more transformative capabilities in the 0.68-0.7 window, warns that AI could speed up pretraining research as soon as 2027, and proposes industry caps that labs would not cross until monitoring catches up. Red SeaRed Sea shipping

  • Saudi coast: the Houthis say they hit Saudi Arabia's Rabigh refinery (Petro Rabigh, about 165 km north of Jeddah). The coalition says it destroyed a drone launched toward the western region, and Saudi authorities have not confirmed any damage. Trump told Fox he is inclined to order strikes on Iran next year if talks fail. CNBC/Reuters
  • JMIC Update 116 (Nov 22): Bab el-Mandeb is still open but "volatile", and there has been no corroborated attack on commercial shipping in the Red Sea since Aug 24. S&P tracking shows about 23 transits a day on Nov 19-21, up from about 19 on Nov 15-18, with 24 cargo-ship transits on Nov 21 alone. The Houthis now hold the Dhubab and Bab el-Mandeb coast that government forces left on Nov 15, which could let them deploy radars and short-range weapons there. In the Gulf of Aden, a skiff with six armed men fired four shots at the bulker DAISY about 95 nm SW of Al Mukalla on Nov 20; guards returned fire and there was no damage. JMIC/UKMTO
  • Gulf spillover: two more outbound tankers, TORKEL and ARINA, were hit by projectiles near Khasab on Nov 21. That makes seven tankers struck in Hormuz since Nov 16, and S&P recorded no cargo transits on Nov 19-21. UKMTO reported another incident in the Gulf of Oman on Sunday, and Bahrain says an Iranian attack injured two people on Saturday. JMIC, CNBC

ScienceScience & CS

  • Dengue: in a four-year randomised study of more than 5,700 children in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, neighbourhoods where Wolbachia-carrying mosquitoes were released had about 69% fewer dengue and chikungunya infections (confirmed by blood tests), and the effect held through the 2024 outbreak. The results were presented Saturday at ASTMH. AP/LA Times
  • BepiColombo entered Mercury orbit on Nov 21 after an eight-year trip, making it only the second mission to orbit the planet. JAXA's MIO probe reaches its orbit on Dec 9, and ESA's MPO descends to 400-1,500 km by March 2027. Nature
  • JWST: MARTA 3887, a clumpy, metal-poor galaxy at z~3.8, appears to host a black hole of about 3.3 million suns, about 2.3% of its stellar mass and roughly 100 times the local ratio. That fits the idea that early black holes grew faster than their hosts. The authors call it a "possible" black hole. Phys.org, arXiv
