ScienceScience & CS

  • Riemann hypothesis: on Nov 20 Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge released a Claude-assisted proof as a 17-million-line Lean library (about six weeks of work, per the Guardian). On Nov 21 the Lean and Mathlib communities, endorsed by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative, said they have "full confidence" it is correct: it compiles, proves Mathlib's exact statement and passes several independent proof checkers. James Maynard called it a likely "historic milestone"; Andrew Granville called the 4-page write-up "garbled", and humans still need to understand the ideas. A separate, human-written draft by Alessandro Masullo appeared the same day and is unverified. Lean community, Nature, Guardian
  • Voyager 1 reached one light-day from Earth (about 16.1 billion miles) on Nov 18, after 49 years; NASA confirmed it via the carrier signal, since no science or engineering data has arrived since Nov 14 and engineers are still analysing that issue. NASA
