ScienceScience & CS

  • arXiv: David Turturean (Physical Superintelligence) proves the 2008 Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture: every bounded degree-d function on the cube has a variable with influence at least c·Var5/2/d6. So T-query quantum algorithms can be classically simulated on most inputs with poly(T) queries. The statements were produced by a non-public Anthropic model, formalized in Lean, and Scott Aaronson referred experts to audit it. arXiv
  • Nature: Intellia's lonvo-z, an in-body CRISPR edit for hereditary angioedema, still works four years after treatment. The FDA decision is due by March 10, and approval would make it the first approved in vivo CRISPR medicine. Nature
